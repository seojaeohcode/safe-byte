# Safe-Byte App Design Concept

Safe-Byte now follows a **dark health-check flow** inspired by the WWIT Planfit reference.

Reference:
- WWIT Planfit: `https://wwit.design/2023/07/24/planfit/`
- Key screens reviewed: onboarding search/select and premium comparison screens.

## Visual Direction

- Dark, focused background instead of warm paper or decorative label grids.
- Oversized question-style headings.
- Mint progress/action color.
- Large high-contrast selection cards.
- Minimal surfaces with restrained borders.
- Result screens use one strong semantic color at a time.

## Palette

- Background `#191B20`
- Surface `#252A32`
- Raised surface `#202329`
- Text `#F7FBFA`
- Muted text `#A7ADB7`
- Border `#3A404B`
- Accent mint `#2DE2C6`
- Info blue `#87A9FF`
- Pass green `#30D989`
- Caution amber `#FFCF66`
- Danger coral `#FF6F61`

## Rules

- No yellowish paper background.
- No decorative label-grid pattern.
- Use mint only for progress, selected states, and primary actions.
- Use large type and short copy; avoid cramped explanatory paragraphs.
- Keep repeated cards at 8px radius for consistency with the app system.
